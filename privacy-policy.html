Sprites & Secrets – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Sprites & Secrets is a pass-and-play game made by Nick McGough. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We don't collect any data

Sprites & Secrets does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app works completely offline and never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

To let you resume a game in progress, the app saves the current game (player names you type, the board and scores) and your sound and vibration settings on your device only. This information never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the app.

Children

Because the app collects no information, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
